# Third-party notices

## heic-to

Seed dynamically loads the unmodified [`heic-to`](https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to) browser package when a user
selects a HEIC or HEIF image. The package includes libheif and is distributed under the
[GNU Lesser General Public License v3.0](https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to/blob/main/LICENSE).

Upstream source: <https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to>

## Noto Sans

Seed fetches [Noto Sans](https://fonts.google.com/noto) font subsets from Google Fonts at render time when generating
Open Graph preview images for documents with non-Latin titles (Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Thai, Arabic, Hebrew,
Devanagari, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, and symbol/math scripts). The fonts are distributed under the
[SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://scripts.sil.org/OFL).

Upstream source: <https://fonts.google.com/noto>

## Twemoji

Seed fetches [Twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji) SVG assets from the jsDelivr CDN at render time when
generating Open Graph preview images for documents whose titles contain emoji. Twemoji is © Twitter/X and contributors
and is distributed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License (CC-BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Upstream source: <https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji>
