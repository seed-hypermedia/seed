**wake source**: whatever ends a [park](./park.md). It is one of: [children](./child.md) finishing, a timer, a **SignalRun** (the card's **Answer** button sends the run's `answerWith` signal), an activity event, or a person pressing **Resume** on a budget pause. [Triggers](./triggers.md) can also wake a run. See the [signed API](./signed-api.md) for `SignalRun`. <!-- id:_M5QUcYg -->
