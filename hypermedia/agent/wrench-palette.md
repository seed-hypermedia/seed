**wrench palette**: the tool button in the chat composer. It lets a person run [read](./read.md), [write](./write.md), and [call](./call.md) themselves. Results land on the [log](./log.md) with a **"You" chip**, and the agent reads them as ground truth (`<user_action>` in its replay). See [desktop UI](./desktop-ui.md). <!-- id:OLGsxfeU -->
