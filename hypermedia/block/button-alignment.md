**Button alignment** is the horizontal position of a [button](./button.md) in its column: `flex-start` (left), `center`, or `flex-end` (right).

# Shape <!-- id:JixrHahr -->

Kind: `string`. One of: `flex-start`, `center`, `flex-end`. <!-- id:F4aUo8h4 -->
