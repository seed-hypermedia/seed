**Authority**: a public key that owns and signs everything under its name. A domain like `hyper.media` resolves to one. The library is published under one authority, the Hypermedia account (`hm://z6MkmZUb…/*`), and its families are told apart by path: the root and `schema/` for the Hypermedia Network's concepts and the type language (see [the Hypermedia chapter](./schema/blobs.md)), `rpc/` for the Seed API's read models, and `example/` for the examples. <!-- id:8jhx0qR- -->
