The app environment is the service that runs [Seed Agents](./agent.md): it keeps each agent's space, sessions and runs, calls language models, executes tools and fires triggers. This section is its reference. [Seed Agents](./agent.md) introduces the runtime for a reader new to it, and [The app environment](./apps/app-environment.md) describes the program, its code and how it ships. <!-- id:KIEbnUjE -->

Every page of the section is listed below. Each term and verb has a page of its own, and the [glossary](./app-environment/glossary.md) defines them all in one line each. <!-- id:UboWw9QH -->
