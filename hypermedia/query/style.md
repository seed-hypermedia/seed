The **query style** is the `style` of a [query block](../block/query.md): `Card` (a grid of cards, `columnCount` wide), `List` (compact rows), or `Table` (columns per attribute, configured by a [table config](./table-config.md)). <!-- id:QoWr4xRS -->

# Shape <!-- id:oLy5zsFB -->

Kind: `string`. One of: `Card`, `List`, `Table`. <!-- id:vpFHkKnX -->
