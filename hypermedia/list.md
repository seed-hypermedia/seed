---
name: List
summary: An ordered sequence, which is List<Any> until it is parameterized with items.
---
The **list** type is an ordered sequence of values, such as `["a", "b", "c"]`. On its own it accepts items of [any](./any.md) type. A [list schema](./schema/list-schema.md) adds `items` to say what each item must be. It is a [primitive](./schema/primitive.md) of the [data model](./schema/data-model.md). The type's formal schema is attached to this page as the `schemaDefinition` in its [metadata](./metadata.md). So the app can show the schema and create values of this type. <!-- id:LXaNf3DO -->
