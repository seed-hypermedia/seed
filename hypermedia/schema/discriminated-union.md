**Discriminated union** — a type that is "one of" a fixed set of variant shapes, told apart by a discriminant. Here the discriminant is the `type` tag (plus "has `anyOf`" / "has `var`" / "`type` names a schema rather than a kind"). Expressed with `anyOf`.
