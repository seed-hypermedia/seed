**Variant**: one of the member schemas of the meta-schema union: `schema/struct-schema`, `schema/map-schema`, `schema/list-schema`, `schema/scalar-schema`, `schema/link-schema`, `schema/include-schema`, `schema/anyof`, `schema/var-schema`, `schema/literal-schema` — each a closed map — plus the bare `string`, `integer`, `boolean` and `null` kinds, which are literals. <!-- id:ndDb3Ur5 -->
