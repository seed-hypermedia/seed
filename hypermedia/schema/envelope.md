**Envelope**: the JSON spelling of a [link](../link.md) or bytes in [dag-json](./dag-json.md), using the reserved `/` key. It spells a distinct kind and is **not** a real map. [Encoding](./encoding.md) has the rules. <!-- id:nVeRWJJj -->
