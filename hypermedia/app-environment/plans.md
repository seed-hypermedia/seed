A plan is a dated design document for the [app environment](../app-environment.md). It says what a change is for and how it would be built. The [roadmap](./roadmap.md) says what is being worked on now, and a plan is the detail behind one roadmap item. Once a plan ships or is dropped it stays here as a record, and its first paragraph says so. <!-- id:zF83xami -->
