---
name: "Agent schedule"
summary: "When a schedule trigger fires: interval, weekly timetable, or a single instant."
---

Three variants: `interval` (every N minutes or hours), `weekly` (days of week + time of day in a timezone), and `once` (a single future instant). Used by [agent-trigger-source](hm://z6Mkqi6bocisxxWzmXSXErC9WpcoonHnpkcogWoSTgoSQTtJ/agent-trigger-source).
