---
name: "Agent tool result"
summary: "A content-addressed record of a tool invocation's outcome."
---

The outcome of one tool call, paired to its [agent-tool-call](hm://z6Mkqi6bocisxxWzmXSXErC9WpcoonHnpkcogWoSTgoSQTtJ/agent-tool-call) by `callId`: the output value or the error, plus `durationMs` and the issuing turn's [usage](hm://z6Mkqi6bocisxxWzmXSXErC9WpcoonHnpkcogWoSTgoSQTtJ/agent-run-usage) when a model turn made the call.
